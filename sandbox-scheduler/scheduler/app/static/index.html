Job states: QUEUED -> ADMITTED -> RUNNING -> SUCCEEDED / FAILED / EXPIRED, or -> CANCELLING -> CANCELLED from anywhere. task jobs run your command inside the sandbox then release it. service jobs keep the sandbox until you cancel or max_runtime hits.
Fairness + aging: tenants take turns (least-served first). A waiting job gains +1 effective priority every few seconds (see the aging bonus tick up live in the queue), so nothing starves. A big job that does not fit is skipped, not blocking smaller ones.
